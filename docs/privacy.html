Mark — Archery Scorecard

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Mark - Archery Scorecard ("Mark," "the app," "we," "us") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. Mark is developed and maintained by an individual developer, Brendan Curiale.

Information We Collect

We collect only what's needed to provide the app's core functionality:

  • Account information: your name and email address, collected when you create an account (either directly, or provided by Google if you sign in with Google).
  • Archery scoring data: the scorecards you create, including round names, end/arrow structure, and the scores you enter.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, or any device data beyond what's required for the app to function.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account, and let you sign in.
  • To store and sync your scorecards across your devices.
  • To send you transactional emails you've requested — for example, email confirmation when you sign up, or a password reset link.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third-Party Services

Mark relies on a small number of third-party services to operate:

Supabase — provides our backend database and authentication. Your account and scorecard data is stored on Supabase's infrastructure. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.
Google Sign-In — if you choose to sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email with us to create your account. See Google's Privacy Policy.
Resend — delivers transactional emails (account confirmation, password reset) on our behalf. See Resend's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase's infrastructure and transmitted using encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your scorecards and account details is restricted to your own account using database-level access controls (Row Level Security) — other users cannot see your data.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account and scorecard data for as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account and all associated data deleted, email us at the address below and we'll process the request.

Your Choices

  • You can edit your name at any time from your profile in the app.
  • You can sign out of the app at any time.
  • You can request a copy of, or the deletion of, your data by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

Mark is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continuing to use the app after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
